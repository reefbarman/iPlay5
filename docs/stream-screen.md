# The stream screen

## Corner shortcuts

The stream keeps the screen clear. Hidden shortcuts sit in the corners, and hints point to them for a few seconds when each stream starts.

| Where               | What                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Top-left corner     | Tap to reveal the PS button, then tap it to press PS                                                                                            |
| Top-right corner    | Tap to reveal the gear, then tap it to open the stream menu                                                                                     |
| Bottom-right corner | Tap to reveal **FG**, tap again to turn [frame generation](picture.md#frame-generation) on or off                                               |
| Bottom-left corner  | Mic mute button when the [microphone](audio.md#microphone) is on, and the frame capture button when [enabled](troubleshooting.md#frame-capture) |

With a controller, holding **Select + Start** for a second also opens the menu. With [virtual controls](touch-and-virtual-controls.md#virtual-controls), Create and Options sit in the black bar below the picture.

## Stream menu

- **Resume**, **Put console in rest mode** (waits for the PS5 to close the connection, then returns to the console list) and **Disconnect**.
- **Mute microphone**, when the mic is on.
- **Phone:** battery level and temperature. When the phone gets hot, a hint suggests 30 fps and **Switch to 30 fps to cool down** does it in one tap.
- **Connection:** [Optimise connection](connection.md#optimise-connection).
- **Virtual controls this session**, if you picked differently when the stream started, with **Use saved virtual controls setting**.
- **All settings.** Changes apply to the running stream. Video and network changes reconnect it, and are saved to the console if it has [its own settings](consoles-and-setup.md#per-console-settings).
- **Controller Test** and **Controls Guide** (under Controller).
- **Screenshots:** see below.
- **Share diagnostics** and **Report a problem on GitHub**, so you can capture a problem without leaving the stream. See [Help and troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#reporting-a-problem).

## Typing on your phone

When the PS5 opens a text box (search, messages, Wi-Fi passwords), a **Type on your phone** box appears with the iOS keyboard. Type and tap **Send**, instead of picking letters on the PS5's keyboard.

## Screenshots

In the stream menu:

- **Marketing screenshot mode** keeps the virtual controls and shortcuts visible while you frame the shot.
- **Save screenshot** saves a PNG of the stream to **Files › On My iPhone › iPlay5** (**On My iPad** on iPad), then returns the controls to normal.

## Screens you might see

- **No controller connected:** turn on or pair a controller and the stream connects as soon as it's found, or choose **Use virtual controls**, **Connect without controls** or **Cancel**.
- **Turn your phone sideways:** on iPhone the stream plays in landscape and connects once you turn. On iPad it plays in any orientation, including Split View and Stage Manager windows, with the picture fitted to the window.
- **Connection lost. Reconnecting…** and **Reconnecting with new settings…:** see [Reconnecting](connection.md#reconnecting).
- **Disconnected:** **Reconnect** or **Close**.
- **Open your phone to play** and **Paused:** iPhone Duo fold states, see [iPhone Duo](iphone-duo.md).

The screen stays at 120 Hz on ProMotion iPhones while streaming (see [Display](picture.md#display)), and the status bar and home indicator stay hidden.
