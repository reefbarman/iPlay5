# iPhone Duo

iPlay5 is tuned for the iPhone Duo's near-square inner screen, and works on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or later.

## Inner and outer screens

- **Inner screen:** the stream plays here, drawn at the panel's native resolution. The app stays in landscape once it's sideways.
- **Outer screen:** portrait. Browse, add, link and wake consoles here. Tap **Play** and iPlay5 shows **Open your phone to play**, then connects as soon as you unfold.
- **Folding mid-game** pauses the picture and sound but keeps the connection open. Unfold to keep playing.
- If iPlay5 ever mistakes the inner screen for the folded one (for example with an unusual Display Zoom setting), **Play here anyway** starts the stream regardless.

On other iPhones, menus are portrait and the stream plays in landscape. On iPad, the app and the stream work in any orientation and window size.

## Controller

The [GameSir G8+](https://gamesir.com/products/g8plus-bluetooth-mobile-controller) **Bluetooth** version clips onto the Duo well. Avoid the Apple-specific (USB-C) G8, which doesn't work with the Duo.

- Pair the G8+ in its iOS mode (blue Home light), where it shows up as "DUOLSHOK 4 Wireless Controller".
- iPlay5 uses its built-in gyro for motion controls and [gyro aiming](controllers.md#gyro-aiming), and its own rumble motors for vibration.
- If its Home button doesn't reach the app, tap the top-left corner of the stream for the on-screen PS button.

## Shim

The G8+'s grip can press the Duo's side button and trigger Siri mid-game. The free [iPhone Duo shim for the GameSir G8+](https://makerworld.com/en/models/3648382-iphone-duo-shim-for-gamesir-g8-no-siri-presses) on MakerWorld stops it.
