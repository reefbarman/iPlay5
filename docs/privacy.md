# Privacy policy

iPlay5 doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer never receives anything from the app unless you choose to send it.

This policy covers iPlay5 however you installed it: from the App Store, TestFlight or by sideloading.

## What stays on your device

- **Consoles and settings:** your linked consoles, their addresses, Remote Play keys and your settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app removes them.
- **Screenshots and frame captures:** saved to the app's folder in the Files app, and only when you take them.

## Who iPlay5 talks to

- **Your PS5:** the stream, your controller input and (if you turn on **Microphone**) your voice go directly between your device and your console, over your local network or the remote address you give it.
- **Sony's sign-in service:** when you tap **Sign in to PSN** while linking a console, you sign in on Sony's own page so iPlay5 can look up the PSN account ID that Remote Play linking needs. iPlay5 keeps the account ID and doesn't keep your password or sign-in token. Sony's [privacy policy](https://www.playstation.com/legal/privacy-policy/) covers that sign-in.

Nothing is sent anywhere else.

## Permissions

- **Local Network:** to find your PS5 and stream from it.
- **Microphone:** only used while **Microphone** is on in settings, to send your voice to the PS5 for game and party chat.

## Diagnostics

**Settings › Share diagnostics** creates a plain-text report only when you tap it, and you choose where it goes (for example, attached to a GitHub issue). IP addresses, keys, account IDs and console names are removed before you see it. Nothing is sent automatically. See [Help and troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Children

iPlay5 doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect data at all.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page, and its history is in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/reefbarman/iPlay5/commits/main/docs/privacy.md). For questions, open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/reefbarman/iPlay5/issues).
