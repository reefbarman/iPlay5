# Audio

## Game audio

The PS5's stereo game audio plays with a very short buffer to keep it in sync with the picture. If playback ever falls behind, it trims itself back to catch up.

Audio comes back on its own after phone calls, Siri, and switching between AirPods, other headphones and the speaker.

PlayStation 3D Audio (as on the PS Portal) isn't supported. The PS5 only offers plain stereo to Remote Play apps like iPlay5.

## Microphone

Settings › Audio › Microphone. Off by default.

- Sends your voice to the PS5 for game and party chat. The first time you turn it on, iOS asks for mic access. If you've said no before, turn it on in iOS **Settings › Apps › iPlay5**.
- A mic button in the bottom-left corner of the stream mutes and unmutes it. The stream menu has **Mute microphone** too.
- On the phone's speaker, echo cancellation stops your party hearing the game. It only runs then, so headphones keep full-quality game audio.
- With AirPods or other Bluetooth headphones the phone's mic is used, unless you're on iOS 26 or later with AirPods that support high-quality recording.
